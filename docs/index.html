Methodology & data

RS line = security close ÷ benchmark close (weekly). JdK RS-Ratio normalizes that line against its own 52-week EMA (×100) — where a security sits versus the benchmark. JdK RS-Momentum is a smoothed 4-week rate-of-change of RS-Ratio (×100) — how fast that standing is changing. Both center on 100.

Quadrants follow the two axes: Leading (strong & rising), Weakening (strong & falling), Lagging (weak & falling), Improving (weak & rising). Capital typically rotates clockwise.

Regime shading on the price strip is a transparent proxy from the benchmark's own weekly closes: bear at ≤ −20% drawdown from the trailing peak, correction at ≤ −8% or below the 40-week average, otherwise bull.

Data via Yahoo Finance, weekly bars over ~3 years, refreshed every Friday. Open source — source on GitHub.

How to read it: the horizontal axis is relative strength (JdK RS-Ratio), the vertical axis its momentum (JdK RS-Momentum), both vs the benchmark and centered at 100. Capital tends to rotate clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging.
